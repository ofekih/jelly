jelly privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

What jelly stores

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo, used to identify your account and show your profile to friends.
  • The spots you rank or bookmark, your ratings, notes and settings, stored in Google Firebase.
  • If you don't sign in, your lists stay in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

What other people can see

  • Your profile: anyone can find you in jelly's friend search by name or handle and see your name, handle, photo, how many spots you've ranked, who you follow and who follows you. Your email address is never shown.
  • Your list: signing in creates a share link. Anyone with that link (including friends who follow you) can see your ranked spots with their scores, notes and favorite dishes, and your bookmarks. Lists can't be browsed or searched; they only open from a link.
  • Community averages: your scores count toward the jelly average shown for each spot, without your name. When you're the only person who ranked a spot, that average is your score.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase for sign-in, storage and hosting.
  • Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics.
  • Google Maps and Places to search for spots and show maps.

jelly never sells your data or shows ads.

Deleting your data

You can delete any spot from within the app. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email ofek.gila@gmail.com.
